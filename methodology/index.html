Methodology

zamindaro combines user inputs, public-record details when available, estimated selling costs, tax assumptions, and long-term scenario math to compare selling with keeping the property.

What is modeled

  • Estimated net proceeds after mortgage payoff, selling costs, and taxes
  • Keep-path monthly view including ownership costs and rental economics
  • Long-term value comparison using appreciation and reinvestment assumptions

What is not guaranteed

These are educational estimates, not appraisals, quotes, tax returns, or legal advice. Every decision should be pressure-tested with local professionals before acting.
