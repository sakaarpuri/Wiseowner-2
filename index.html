🧮 Depreciation (Schedule E) — optional

US landlords can deduct the building's depreciation each year on IRS Schedule E. Residential buildings depreciate over 27.5 years — it reduces your taxable rental income on paper, but the IRS recaptures it at sale (see §1250).

$
You can only depreciate the structure — not the land. Building value is typically 70–85% of total value. So a $600k property → ~$420k building. A cost segregation study or appraiser can confirm the split.
$
Divide your building value by 27.5. E.g. $400k building → ~$14,545/yr. Leave at $0 to skip — but note the IRS may still assume depreciation was taken when you sell.
⚠️
Depreciation recapture: The IRS taxes recaptured depreciation at 25% (§1250) when you sell — even if you didn't claim it. This is separate from capital gains tax. Talk to a CPA before deciding whether to claim it.
