Is Now a Good Time to Sell?

The market headline is rarely the full answer. The real question is whether selling now produces a better outcome than keeping the property once your taxes, carrying costs, and alternatives are included.

Why market timing is not the whole story

Most homeowners do not sell in a vacuum. They are also moving, buying, investing, simplifying, or protecting the Section 121 clock. Those practical realities usually matter more than trying to guess the perfect month.

Signals that selling now may make sense

  • You have meaningful equity to unlock
  • The property is expensive to carry
  • The main-home exclusion could be lost if you wait too long
  • You do not want to be a landlord
  • You need liquidity or a simpler balance sheet

Signals that waiting may be better

  • You are close to qualifying for a larger tax break
  • The keep path has strong long-term upside
  • You would have to buy right back into the same expensive market
  • Your local appreciation outlook still looks strong

The five-question framework

  1. What would you actually net if you sold today?
  2. What would that money do elsewhere?
  3. What does the next 10 years look like if you keep the property?
  4. What does your life need now: liquidity, stability, or simplicity?
  5. What is the tax cost of waiting?
Use your own numbers, not headlines.
