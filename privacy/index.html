Privacy

zamindaro is designed to give homeowners an estimate using the numbers they provide and public property data when available. We do not position the product as a substitute for professional tax, legal, or financial advice.

Information used

Inputs you provide, account information for authenticated users, and external property-market data used to support the estimate.

Use of information

To generate the report, support accounts and subscriptions, improve the product, and provide support when requested.
